Homes in Epsom

Epsom has inter-war semi-detached and detached family houses, Victorian homes near the town centre, and newer developments. Family bathrooms and en-suites added to main bedrooms are the most common projects.

Epsom work is usually complete family bathrooms, new en-suites and refits of existing en-suites.

Things we check first

  • Inter-war houses often have a separate WC next to the bathroom, which can be combined into one better room.
  • Adding an en-suite to the main bedroom depends on the drainage route to the soil stack and on ventilation.
  • Newer homes may already have en-suites that are dated and ready for a complete refit.
  • Most homes have off-street parking, which keeps deliveries and waste removal simple.

Parking and access

Epsom is in Epsom & Ewell Borough. Most properties have their own parking, so we just confirm access for deliveries.

We visit to survey, measure and check the services, then give you a written quote. Bring photographs to the shop at 287 Munster Road if you would like to talk it through first.
