Nicholas · Our Cobham connection

Fulham roots.
A second home in Cobham.

My connection to Cobham goes back to the early 2000s, when my mum, Barbara, and my stepdad, Alan, moved from Fulham to a home near the station. Since then, Cobham has become a second home to me.

Today, I’m bringing that personal connection together with my work at En-Suites & Bathrooms. Our shop remains in Fulham, and I also work from Cobham, serving homeowners across Elmbridge and surrounding Surrey with bathroom renovations and new en-suite installations.

Nicholas

Practical experience, from the start.

Nicholas began plumbing in 1998 and brings over 25 years of hands-on plumbing and building experience to the business. He discusses your ideas, surveys the room and helps plan the practical route through the installation.

The business brings that practical experience together with a personal approach: understanding what you want, explaining the work and agreeing the details before installation.

Two bases. One company.

Our Cobham base supports home surveys and installation work across Elmbridge, Leatherhead and nearby Surrey. We visit you to look at the room and discuss the project.

Our shop at 287 Munster Road, Fulham, London SW6 6BW remains available for consultations. Please call ahead to arrange a visit. Surrey enquiries come directly to the same business, with the same attention to planning and workmanship.
