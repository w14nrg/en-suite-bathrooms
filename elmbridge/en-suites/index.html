Plan the space you have

A private bathroom starts with a workable layout.

The first step is to understand the room and the services around it. We assess the space before asking you to commit to a design.

Bedroom

Keep the bedroom usable.

We consider the bed, storage, windows and door openings together with the shower, toilet and basin. The remaining bedroom matters just as much as the new room.

Loft

Work with the roof shape.

Sloping ceilings, headroom and access affect where fittings can go. We look at the practical layout and the route to existing services.

Existing en-suite

Improve what is already there.

A better shower, revised layout or complete renovation can make an awkward en-suite easier to use and maintain.

Before choosing fittings

Check what happens behind the walls.

A new en-suite brings plumbing and building work into a space that may never have had either.

  • Drainage routes and the connection to the soil pipe
  • Water supply and the shower’s requirements
  • Ventilation and electrical work
  • Floor and wall preparation, waterproofing and tiling
  • Room access and the space left around the fittings

A clear proposal

What will my en-suite cost?

Cost depends on the size, plumbing route, preparation and products. A room next to existing services can involve different work from one further away.

After discussing your ideas and surveying the space, we set out the included work in a written quotation. Any additional work is discussed and agreed.

Before you book

Your questions, answered.

Do I need a spare bedroom?

No. We assess whether part of an existing bedroom or loft can be used while leaving a practical bedroom layout.

Can you tell from a photograph whether it will fit?

Photos and approximate dimensions are a useful start. A survey checks the exact layout, service routes and preparation needed before the design is agreed.

Do you cover Leatherhead as well as Elmbridge?

Yes. We welcome en-suite projects in Leatherhead, Fetcham, Bookham and other nearby Surrey areas, alongside Elmbridge.

Can I choose my own fittings?

Yes. We can plan around products you like, checking their dimensions, compatibility and installation requirements before the scope is agreed.
