Before you book

Your questions, answered.

Can you replace a bath with a shower?

Yes. We assess the available space, floor levels, drainage and the type of shower you want before proposing the work.

Do you renovate bathrooms outside Elmbridge?

Yes. Our Cobham base also serves Leatherhead and nearby Surrey. Send your postcode if you are unsure whether we cover your area.

Can you renovate a bathroom and add an en-suite?

Yes. Tell us about both rooms so we can consider their layouts, services and programme together.

How long will the work take?

The programme depends on the agreed scope, preparation and product availability. We discuss the expected timing when preparing your proposal.
