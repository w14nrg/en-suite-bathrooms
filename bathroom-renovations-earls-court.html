BATHROOM RENOVATIONS · EARL’S COURT SW5

Bathroom Renovations in Earl’s Court

Earl’s Court is a short drive from Munster Road. Many of its large Victorian houses are now flats and rented rooms, which shapes how bathrooms and en-suites are planned.

WhatsApp Nicholas

Homes in Earl’s Court

Earl’s Court is known for tall stucco-fronted Victorian houses, most now divided into flats, with a good number let as shared houses or studios. That brings a steady need for durable bathrooms and for en-suites that give a rented room its own facilities.

Earl’s Court work is often a flat bathroom renovation for an owner, or an en-suite or shower room upgrade for a landlord.

Things we check first

  • Rented flats need hard-wearing finishes: good-quality shower valves, solid screens and easily cleaned surfaces.
  • Adding an en-suite to a rented room depends on the drainage route and ventilation, and sometimes on building regulations sign-off.
  • Upper floors in tall houses mean long carries of materials and waste, which we price and plan for.
  • Shared stacks and older lead or iron pipework are common, so we check what the new fittings will connect to.

Parking and access

Earl’s Court is in the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea, which has its own parking and skip-licence rules. We book what is needed before the start date.

We visit to survey, measure and check the services, then give you a written quote. Bring photographs to the shop at 287 Munster Road if you would like to talk it through first.

Questions

Do you work for landlords?

Yes. We can plan bathrooms and en-suites for rented rooms and flats, keeping finishes durable and the programme short to limit empty days.

Can an en-suite add value to a rented room?

It can make a room more appealing, but it depends on the room size you lose and local demand. Our spare-room en-suite page explains the trade-offs.

Kitchens, renovations and building work in Earl’s Court

In Earl’s Court flats we regularly combine a shower room with plastering, decorating and new lighting.

Kitchens

New kitchens from the bare walls up, with the electrics, plumbing, worktops, tiling and flooring all done by our own team. We plan the layout around where the services can run, then fit and finish it as one job.

Kitchens in more detail

Part and full renovations

If you are doing more than one room, such as a bathroom, kitchen and decorating, we plan it as one job with one programme. Many Earl’s Court flats are renovated between tenants, so we plan the work to get the property back on the market without loose ends.

Part and full renovations in more detail

Tiling

Wall and floor tiling for bathrooms, kitchens and hallways, in porcelain, ceramic, stone and mosaic. Older walls in these houses are often uneven, so we prepare and board them properly before tiling.

Tiling in more detail

Electrical work

From a few extra sockets and new downlights to a full rewire and new consumer unit. Converted period houses often have wiring added in stages over the years, so we test and trace it before adding to it.

Electrical work in more detail

Painting and decorating

Rooms, hallways and stairwells, with filling, sanding and preparation done properly before painting. We often redecorate whole flats between lets, so they are ready to show.

Painting and decorating in more detail

Carpentry

Internal carpentry: new doors, frames and skirting, stud walls, boxing-in and fitted shelving. In converted flats we often build stud walls and box in pipework as part of a new layout.

Carpentry in more detail

Plastering

Plastering and skimming, from making good after other trades to whole rooms. Tall period ceilings and old lath and plaster often need re-boarding rather than patching.

Plastering in more detail

Flooring

Engineered wood, LVT, laminate and tiled floors, with the subfloor checked and levelled first. We can clad the stairs to match the new floor.
