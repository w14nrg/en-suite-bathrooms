Order of work

  1. Walk round togetherRoom by room, writing down what each one needs.
  2. Strip-outOnly the rooms in the scope.
  3. ServicesWiring and pipework while walls and floors are open.
  4. Walls and floorsPlastering, boarding and levelling.
  5. Kitchen and bathroomsFitted, tiled and connected.
  6. Floors, decorating and handoverRooms checked with you as each one is finished.

You get a written programme with your quote, so you know the order of work before we start.

Questions

What is the difference between part and full?

Part is a chosen set of rooms; full is the whole property. Both are planned and priced as one job.

Can I live in the property during a renovation?

During a part renovation, usually yes. A full renovation is quicker and cleaner if the property is empty.

How is it priced?

After a survey, as one quotation with each room broken down.
