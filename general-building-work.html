The right person for the right work

Electrical and gas work must be handled properly

Some general building jobs are straightforward. Others touch electrical work, gas work, ventilation or building regulations. When that happens, the right qualified trade needs to be involved.

For electrical work, a registered competent person can self-certify certain types of building work instead of the homeowner applying separately for building regulations approval. For gas work, the engineer must be on the Gas Safe Register. We keep this clear from the start so the work is approached safely and properly.

Registered electricians used where required
Gas Safe engineers used for gas work
Building regulation considerations discussed where relevant
Certificates and compliance routes considered before work starts

Electrical

Lighting, sockets, fans, mirrors and electrical changes need the right route, especially in kitchens and bathrooms.

Gas

Any gas appliance or gas fitting work must be carried out by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

Ventilation

Extractor fans, ducting and ventilation routes need proper planning in bathrooms, cloakrooms and kitchens.

Paperwork

Where certification is needed, it should be thought about before the work starts, not chased afterwards.

Questions

General building work FAQs

Do you only take on big jobs?

No. We can help with smaller jobs as well as more involved work. If it is connected to improving, repairing or finishing the home, it is worth asking.

Can I send photos first?

Yes. Photos, videos and product links are often the quickest way to start. Some jobs can be understood from photos, while others will need a site visit.

Do you use qualified electricians and Gas Safe engineers?

Yes. Where the work requires a qualified or registered specialist, the correct trade is used. Electrical and gas work must be handled properly and safely.

Can you coordinate more than one trade?

Yes. That is often where we are most useful. A job may need plumbing, tiling, decorating and making good in the right order. We can help bring that together.

Can I pop into the shop and ask?

Yes. The Munster Road studio is there so local customers can speak to someone properly, show photos, ask questions and work out the next step.
