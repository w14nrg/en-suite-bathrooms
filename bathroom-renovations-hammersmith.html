What we check first

The hidden details decide how good the bathroom will be.

Before pricing or finalising a bathroom renovation, the practical details need to be understood. Waste runs, soil pipe positions, floor levels, boxing, ventilation routes and hot water supply can all affect the design.

For Hammersmith projects, we can help with full rip-out and installation, shower rooms, en-suites, cloakrooms, bath-to-shower changes, tiling, kitchen splashbacks and selected smaller bathroom works.

Water & drainage

Waste routes, soil connections, water pressure and hot water supply checked early.

Ventilation

Floors & walls
