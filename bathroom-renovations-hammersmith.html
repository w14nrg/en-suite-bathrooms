Homes in Hammersmith

Most of our Hammersmith enquiries come from three kinds of home: Victorian and Edwardian terraces around Brackenbury Village and Ravenscourt Park, red-brick mansion flats off Fulham Palace Road, and newer riverside apartments. Each needs a different plan before anything is ordered.

Typical Hammersmith work is a full family bathroom replaced in a terrace, a tired shower room in a flat brought up to date, or a bath swapped for a walk-in shower where the room is too narrow for both.

Things we check first

  • Terraces often have the bathroom in a narrow rear addition, so the layout has to work around one window and a soil stack on the outside wall.
  • Mansion flats share risers with the flats above and below, so the waterproofing and any change to the waste route need extra care.
  • Riverside blocks usually have a concierge, booked lift slots and rules on noise and working hours that set the programme.
  • Lofts in the terraces can take a compact en-suite once head height and the drainage route down through the house are checked.

Parking and access

Hammersmith & Fulham Council runs controlled parking across W6, so we plan for parking bays or visitor permits and skip licences before the strip-out day, not on it.

We visit to survey, measure and check the services, then give you a written quote. Bring photographs to the shop at 287 Munster Road if you would like to talk it through first.

Questions

Can you work in a mansion flat with strict building rules?

Yes. We work to the managing agent’s hours and protection rules, and we can provide the method statement and insurance details they usually ask for before work starts.

How quickly can you survey in Hammersmith?

Because the shop is close by, a survey in W6 can usually be arranged quickly.

Kitchens, renovations and building work in Hammersmith

In Hammersmith terraces we are often asked to do the kitchen in the rear addition and redecorate once the bathroom above is finished.

Kitchens

We strip out the old kitchen, run new electrics and plumbing, and fit the units, worktops and sink, including Silestone and quartz worktops and undermount sinks. In mansion-block flats the kitchen is often narrow, so layout and storage are planned carefully, and we work to the building’s rules on noise and access.

Part and full renovations

A part renovation (say a bathroom, a kitchen and redecorating) or a full renovation of the whole property, planned and run as one job. One team, one point of contact and one programme from start to finish.

Part and full renovations in more detail

Tiling

Tiling for whole bathrooms, shower areas, kitchen splashbacks and floors. In newer flats we work with the existing floor build-up and the building’s rules on noise.

Tiling in more detail

Electrical work

Full and part rewires, new consumer units, extra sockets, downlights and extractor fans. Older terraces often still have dated wiring in places, which we find and replace while walls and floors are open.

Electrical work in more detail

Painting and decorating

Interior decorating: walls, ceilings, woodwork and doors, prepared properly first. In mansion flats we protect shared areas and keep to the building’s working hours.

Painting and decorating in more detail

Carpentry

Doors, skirting, architrave, stud walls and boxing-in, plus stair cladding. Much of it goes alongside our bathroom, kitchen and renovation work.

Carpentry in more detail

Plastering

Patching after rewires and new pipework, skimming tired walls and ceilings, and re-boarding where needed. Old lath and plaster ceilings are common in these houses, and where they have failed we re-board and skim.

Plastering in more detail

Flooring

New floors in wood, LVT, laminate or tile, laid on a properly prepared subfloor. Old floorboards are often uneven or loose, so we fix and level them before the new floor goes down.
