Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can any bedroom have an en-suite?+

No. A suitable route for drainage, ventilation and services is essential, and the remaining bedroom must still be usable. We assess this before design.

How much space is needed?+

Very compact en-suites are possible, but comfortable access, shower size, door position and maintenance space matter more than one headline dimension.

Can an en-suite increase rent?+

It may. Private facilities can command a premium in some London room markets, but the uplift varies by property, location, room size and current demand. See our dedicated spare-room page for a careful explanation.
