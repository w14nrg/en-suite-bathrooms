TILING

Tiling in Fulham

Wall and floor tiling for bathrooms, kitchens, splashbacks and floors, set out properly on a sound, flat base.

WhatsApp Nicholas

From our shop at 287 Munster Road, Fulham. We also cover Hammersmith, Parsons Green, Chelsea, Kensington, Putney, Wandsworth, Chiswick, Richmond, Wimbledon and more. All areas

Tiling is the part of a room everyone sees, and the part that shows every shortcut underneath it. Most of the job is preparation: stripping back, boarding, levelling and waterproofing before the first tile goes on.

Our tiling is done by Mykhailo, who tiles our bathrooms and kitchens every week. Because we also do the plumbing, carpentry and plastering, problems found behind old tiles get fixed rather than covered.

See it on a real job: Tiling was part of one of our full renovations, with the kitchen, bathroom and every room done as one job. See the full renovation

MosaicNiches, shower floors and feature stripsMore grout lines, so more cleaning; good for falls in a shower floor

What goes behind the tiles

Most tiling problems start underneath. These are the layers we put in, from the wall out.

Tile, grout and siliconeWhat you see
AdhesiveChosen for the tile and the room

Patterns

Straight

Tiles in a grid. Clean and simple, with the least waste.

Brick or offset

Each row shifted. Classic for walls and metro tiles.

Herringbone

Zig-zag pattern for floors and feature walls. More cuts and more time.

Diagonal

Set at 45°. Can make a small floor feel larger; more waste at the edges.

Questions

Can you tile over my old tiles?

In wet areas we do not recommend it. Removing them lets us check and repair what is underneath.

Do you tile kitchens as well as bathrooms?

Yes: splashbacks, walls and floors.

Can you supply the tiles?

Yes, or we can fit tiles you have chosen once we have checked they suit the room.
