New en-suite value

A new private bathroom is a building project.

The cost is not just the shower, toilet and basin. It can include walls, doors, drainage, water supplies, ventilation, electrics, waterproofing, tiling, heating, flooring and making good the bedroom.

Questions

Price questions

Why can’t you give an exact online price?+

The same products can cost very different amounts to install depending on drainage, preparation, access, floor condition, electrical work and the property itself.

Are products included?+

The quotation will state what is supplied, what is an allowance and what is customer supplied. We can work with products you have chosen after checking suitability.

Do you charge for a home survey?+

Initial local home surveys are offered free for suitable planned projects. Wider regional surveys may be discussed depending on distance and project size.

Get a project-specific quotation
