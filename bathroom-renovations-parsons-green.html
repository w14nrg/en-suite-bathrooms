Bathroom Renovations in Parsons Green

Parsons Green is on our doorstep. You can walk into the Munster Road shop to talk through your bathroom, and we can usually survey quickly.

Homes in Parsons Green

Parsons Green is mostly Victorian terraced housing, and a large share of those houses have had the loft converted. That gives us two recurring jobs: the main first-floor bathroom, and an en-suite squeezed into the new top floor.

Most Parsons Green work is a complete family bathroom or a loft shower room, often alongside a bath-to-shower change in a second bathroom.

Things we check first

  • First-floor bathrooms in these terraces are often long and narrow, so the bath, WC and basin positions decide whether a separate shower will fit.
  • Loft en-suites sit under a slope. Shower position, headroom and how the waste reaches the soil stack are checked at survey, not on the day.
  • Older properties can hide failing studwork or cut joists behind tiles. On Rosaline Road nearby we found a joist cut out under the toilet and replaced it.
  • Original floorboards and lath walls need protecting or properly boarding before any tiling goes on.

Parking and access

Parsons Green is within Hammersmith & Fulham’s controlled parking, so van parking and any skip licence are booked with the council before the job starts.

We visit to survey, measure and check the services, then give you a written quote. Bring photographs to the shop at 287 Munster Road if you would like to talk it through first.

Yes. Our Rosaline Road project in Fulham was a loft en-suite under a sloping ceiling, including structural repair to the floor.

Kitchens, renovations and building work in Parsons Green

Around Parsons Green many jobs combine a bathroom with new flooring and decorating before a family moves in.

Kitchens

We strip out the old kitchen, run new electrics and plumbing, and fit the units, worktops and sink, including Silestone and quartz worktops and undermount sinks. In Parsons Green terraces the kitchen is usually in the back addition, which often needs new pipework and better lighting. Side-return and back-addition kitchens are the usual layout here.

Kitchens in more detail

Part and full renovations

A part renovation (say a bathroom, a kitchen and redecorating) or a full renovation of the whole property, planned and run as one job. Where a loft has been converted, we often do the new top-floor en-suite and the main bathroom in the same job.

Part and full renovations in more detail

Tiling

Tiling for whole bathrooms, shower areas, kitchen splashbacks and floors. In older houses we check the floor and walls are sound and flat before any tile goes down, as that is what stops tiles cracking later.

Tiling in more detail

Electrical work

Full and part rewires, new consumer units, extra sockets, downlights and extractor fans. Older terraces often still have dated wiring in places, which we find and replace while walls and floors are open.

Electrical work in more detail

Plumbing

Parsons Green is one of the South-West London areas where we take on plumbing work: new boilers, radiators, pipework, and the plumbing for bathrooms and kitchens. Older terraces often have pipework that has been added to over decades, which we tidy up and replace where it is needed.

Plumbing in more detail

Painting and decorating

Interior decorating: walls, ceilings, woodwork and doors, prepared properly first. Tall stairwells and hallways are common in Parsons Green houses, and we have the access equipment for them.

Painting and decorating in more detail

Carpentry

Doors, skirting, architrave, stud walls and boxing-in, plus stair cladding. Stud walls for new en-suites and boxing-in pipework are regular jobs here. Many Parsons Green loft conversions need new doors, skirting and boxing-in to finish the top floor properly.

Carpentry in more detail

Plastering

Patching after rewires and new pipework, skimming tired walls and ceilings, and re-boarding where needed. Old lath and plaster ceilings are common in these houses, and where they have failed we re-board and skim.

Plastering in more detail

Flooring

New floors in wood, LVT, laminate or tile, laid on a properly prepared subfloor. Old floorboards are often uneven or loose, so we fix and level them before the new floor goes down. Hallways in these terraces are narrow and long, so we often lay the floor and clad the stairs in one go.
