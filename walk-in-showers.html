One of our walk-in showers

The customer no longer wanted his steam shower cabin and asked for something as close to a walk-in shower as possible, without rebuilding the floor.

  • Steam cabin removed and replaced with a low black tray and a black-framed fixed glass screen, giving an open, walk-in feel.
  • Removing the cabin left pipework exposed, so we built a false wall to hide it.
  • The false wall became a feature: dark porcelain, a concealed thermostatic valve and a large mirrored niche.
  • Rainfall head on a black arm plus a handset on a riser rail; wall-hung WC with concealed cistern, floating vanity and black towel radiator.

How we work

A walk-in shower can be built on a low-profile tray or a tiled former. Whether a truly level entrance is possible depends on the floor construction and the waste route, so we check those before promising a flush finish. Our steam-cabin conversion shows how much more open a room can feel without rebuilding the floor.

We start with a conversation and a survey, then give you a written quote matched to the work actually needed.

Questions

Can I have a completely level floor?

Sometimes. It depends on the floor type, the depth available and the drainage route. Where it is not practical, a low tray gives almost the same feel.

Will water splash out without a door?

A correctly sized fixed screen and the right shower head position keep water in. We plan screen width around the room.
