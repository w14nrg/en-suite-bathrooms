About us

Bathrooms designed with an understanding of the build.

A Fulham-based, builder-led business combining practical installation knowledge with bathroom and en-suite planning.

Book a free home surveyWhatsApp your project

Builder-led

Practical experience behind the design.

En-Suites & Bathrooms Ltd was created to give homeowners a clearer route from an idea to a completed room. The business is led by Nick, who began plumbing in 1998 and brings more than 25 years of hands-on building, plumbing and bathroom experience.

The family’s London building roots go back to 1962, when Nick’s father and uncles came to London and worked in the trade. That history is part of the background, while the current company is a separate modern business based in Fulham.
