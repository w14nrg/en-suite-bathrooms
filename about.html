En-Suites & Bathrooms Ltd was created to give homeowners a clearer route from an idea to a completed room. The business is led by Nick, who began plumbing in 1998 and brings more than 25 years of hands-on building, plumbing and bathroom experience.

Family building roots

A Fulham trade background going back generations.

Nick’s father Joe and his brothers Stan and Bob came to London from Bradford in 1962. Joe was a bricklayer, Stan was a plasterer and Bob had an engineering background. Their work developed from major London building projects into property renovation through the family business, Armour Trading Building Co.

Joe spent many years renovating and improving houses across Fulham and West London, including long-standing work connected with local property developer John Tobin and Wilton Trust. Nick grew up around those properties and began working in the trade himself at 16, learning how older London homes are actually built and where renovation problems tend to hide.

That history is not used as a slogan. It explains why En-Suites & Bathrooms approaches design from the build backwards: drainage, pipe routes, floors, walls, preparation and access are considered before the final specification is agreed.

The business today

The family connection continues in a modern bathroom business.

En-Suites & Bathrooms is a separate modern company built from that background. Nick surveys projects, helps shape the design, checks the practical installation route and remains responsible for the important decisions throughout the work.
