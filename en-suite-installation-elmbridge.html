What we check before saying yes

A good en-suite starts with the hidden work.

The shower, basin and tiles are only the visible part. The layout depends on whether the property can support the drainage, water, ventilation and construction properly.

Remaining room size

We measure the proposed en-suite and the bedroom left around it, including door movement and usable furniture space.

Drainage & water

Soil stack position, waste falls, hot water capacity, pressure and pipe routes can decide where the room should go.

Ventilation

A new wet room needs a proper extraction route, not an afterthought once the walls have already been built.

Complete build

Walls, preparation, waterproofing, plumbing, electrics, tiling, sanitaryware and finishing are planned as one job.

Specialist en-suite planning

Creating a new room is different from replacing an old bathroom.

A new en-suite can involve forming a room, finding a soil route, protecting bedroom space, adding ventilation and coordinating several trades. We plan those practical decisions together before the installation begins.
