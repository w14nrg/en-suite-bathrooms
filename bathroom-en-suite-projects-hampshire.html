Planning projects further away

Hampshire is beyond our routine London working area. We therefore consider only projects with a clear, substantial scope and enough advance planning to make survey, coordination and site attendance sensible.

Scope first

Room purpose, layout direction, products, access and responsibilities need to be documented clearly.

Products organised

Long-lead items, deliveries, damaged-goods checks and storage should be resolved before the installation programme.

Programme controlled

Trade sequencing and decision deadlines reduce avoidable return journeys and delays.

Areas we cover in Hampshire

Location alone does not decide acceptance. A complete bathroom, a newly formed en-suite, multiple rooms or work forming part of a wider planned renovation is more likely to be suitable than a minor upgrade.

We remain transparent that the company and consultation office are in Fulham. The regional page exists so owners with a worthwhile project can find and assess the service—not to create a false local presence.

Projects we travel for

This distinction protects both the customer and the project from disproportionate travel and management costs.

More suitable

  • Full bathroom renovation
  • New bedroom or loft en-suite
  • Two or more bathrooms
  • Bathroom work within a broader refurbishment

Usually unsuitable

  • Single tap or shower replacement
  • Minor silicone or grout work
  • Small isolated repair
  • Urgent attendance or diagnosis

What to send us

For a Hampshire enquiry, the first review should establish whether there is a realistic technical and commercial fit.

Postcode

Confirms travel, access and whether the location is practical for the proposed programme.

Wide photographs

Shows room proportions, doors, windows and adjoining areas rather than only the existing suite.

Approximate sizes

Helps identify whether the layout ambition is broadly realistic before precise survey measurements.

Budget range

Lets us discuss whether the scope and finish can align before either side commits time.

Frequently asked questions

Why do you only consider larger Hampshire projects?

Travel, survey and project management need to remain proportionate. A full bathroom, new en-suite, multiple rooms or wider refurbishment provides enough scope for a properly managed regional project.

Which Hampshire areas can you consider?

Enquiries around Fleet, Farnborough, Basingstoke and Winchester can be reviewed, alongside other locations where the proposed scope and programme are practical.

Do you provide urgent or small-job attendance in Hampshire?

No. The Hampshire service is for planned projects, not emergencies, minor repairs or isolated product replacements.

Where is the company based?

The office and consultation base is at 287 Munster Road, Fulham, London SW6 6BW. We do not claim a Hampshire branch.
