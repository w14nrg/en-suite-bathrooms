Chelsea property planning

Local property details change the way the room should be planned.

A bathroom in a period terrace, mansion block or modern apartment can look similar on a moodboard but require a very different installation route.

Townhouse projects can involve multiple floors, difficult material handling and older service routes.
Mansion flats may require building approval, lift protection and careful neighbour consideration.
High-end products should be checked for lead times, compatibility and future maintenance.
Compact guest bathrooms and cloakrooms benefit from restrained, proportionate design.
