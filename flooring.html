Which floor for which room?

The right floor depends on the room as much as the look.

RoomGood choicesWorth knowing
KitchenPorcelain tiles, LVTAvoid solid wood near the sink
BathroomPorcelain tiles, bathroom-rated LVTLaminate is not suitable for wet rooms
HallwayPorcelain tiles, engineered wood, LVTChoose a hard-wearing finish for the busiest floor
Bedrooms and living roomsEngineered wood, laminateA good underlay makes a big difference to noise
Over underfloor heatingTiles, LVT, engineered wood rated for heatingCheck the floor maker's temperature limits

What we check underneath

  • Loose or squeaking boards, screwed down or replaced.
  • Levels, with low spots filled and high spots taken down.
  • Damp, especially on ground floors.
  • Door heights, so doors still swing after the new floor goes down.
