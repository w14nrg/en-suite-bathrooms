What makes the project a good fit?

A clear overall objective helps us decide whether the project should move to survey.

Complete bathroom

Existing room stripped, prepared and rebuilt with coordinated plumbing, tiling, electrics and finishing.

Bedroom en-suite

A new room formed with walls, drainage, ventilation, waterproofing and a practical bedroom layout.

Shower-room redesign

Bath removal or a new walk-in arrangement where floor levels and waste routes are checked first.

Multiple-room scope

Two or more bathrooms or an en-suite alongside wider planned renovation work can make regional coordination more efficient.

Before we travel

Send enough information for a useful first conversation.

Photographs, the postcode, approximate room dimensions and the type of work help us avoid wasting your time with a survey for a project that is too small or technically unsuitable.

Show the whole room

Include doors, windows, floors and adjoining spaces—not only close-ups of products you like.

Explain nearby services

Tell us whether there is a bathroom, soil stack or outside wall nearby if you are proposing a new en-suite.

Discuss budget and finish

A realistic range helps decide whether to explore practical, premium or more extensive design routes.

Frequently asked questions

Which parts of Surrey do you cover?+

We consider suitable projects in places including Kingston, Richmond, Wimbledon, Esher, Cobham, Weybridge, Epsom, Guildford and Woking. The exact location and project size are assessed before a survey.

Do you have a Surrey showroom?+

No. Our real office and consultation base is at 287 Munster Road, Fulham. We extend the service to selected Surrey projects rather than claiming a branch we do not have.

Will you take on one small bathroom upgrade?+

Possibly close to the London boundary, but further travel is generally more appropriate for full renovations, new en-suites, multiple rooms or other substantial planned work.

Can you design around products I have already chosen?+

Yes. We check dimensions, compatibility, delivery and installation needs before incorporating customer-selected products into the scope.
