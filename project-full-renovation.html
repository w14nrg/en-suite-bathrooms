PROJECT · FULL RENOVATION

Full renovation

A complete renovation of a whole property: stripped right back, then new electrics and plumbing, a new kitchen and bathroom, new door frames, plastering throughout, new radiators, new flooring and the stairs clad to match.

What we did

  • Every room stripped back: old wall coverings, kitchen, bathroom, door frames and floor coverings all removed.
  • New electrics and plumbing run throughout while the floors were up.
  • Walls and ceilings plastered and skimmed in every room, with new door frames fitted.
  • New kitchen and new bathroom fitted, plus new radiators.
  • New wood flooring laid, the stairs clad to match with new treads, risers and nosings, and the rooms decorated.
