Bathroom-focused, not general odd jobs

Small jobs still need proper planning

A simple toilet change, basin swap, shower screen installation or tiling job can become awkward if the walls, floors, pipework, waste position, surfaces or existing fittings have not been considered properly.

Because we specialise in bathrooms, en-suites and tiled finishes, we look at the practical details before agreeing what is sensible. That helps avoid forcing unsuitable products or tiles into a room or turning a small job into a messy one.

Useful for landlords and rental properties
Good for tired bathrooms, kitchen splashbacks and tile areas that need a cleaner finish
Can lead into a larger bathroom plan if the room needs more work

Send photos

A few clear photos often help us understand the existing taps, toilet, basin, pipework or shower area.

Check access

Access behind panels, under basins and around toilets can affect what is possible and how long it may take.

Plumbing matters

Water pressure, isolation valves, waste runs and existing pipework all need to be considered before fitting new items.

Clear next step

We will let you know whether it sounds suitable as a smaller job or whether a bigger bathroom plan is needed.
