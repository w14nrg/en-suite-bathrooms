Planning your sockets

The best time to add sockets is during other work, when walls are already open.

  • Mark where furniture will go first: beds, sofas, desks and the TV wall.
  • Put sockets on both sides of a bed.
  • Think about chargers, routers and smart speakers.
  • In kitchens, spread sockets along the worktop, not all in one corner.
  • Add an outdoor socket if you use garden tools or lights.

A rewire, room by room

  1. Check what is thereExisting wiring, the fuse board and what each room needs.
  2. Mark positionsSockets, switches and lights marked on the walls with you.
  3. First fixCables run while floors and walls are open, one area at a time.
  4. Second fix and testFittings on, every circuit tested, walls made good.

You get a written programme with your quote, so you know the order of work before we start.

Questions

Do I need to move out for a rewire?

Not for a part rewire. For a full rewire it is easier if rooms are empty, and we work room by room.

Can you add sockets without damaging the decoration?

We keep chasing to a minimum, but some making good and painting is usually needed, which we also do.

Do you do small electrical jobs?

Yes: new sockets, lights or an extractor fan are all fine.
