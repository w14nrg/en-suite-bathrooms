What the result means

Useful before survey. Verified after survey.

The planner narrows the likely scope and budget. It cannot see joists, soil routes, hidden damage, pipe condition or exact measurements.

Measurements

Room dimensions, doors, windows, clearances and product sizes are checked properly on site.

Water & drainage

Pressure, hot-water performance, waste falls and soil-pipe routes shape the final design.

Floors & walls

Structure, reinforcement, waterproofing and preparation cannot be confirmed from a form alone.

Final proposal

The home survey leads to a buildable specification, inclusions and written quotation.

Questions about the planner

Before you start

How accurate is the estimate?

It is intended to establish a sensible early budget range. Accuracy improves with complete answers, photographs and product information, but a final quotation still requires a home survey.

Can I add products from different retailers?

Yes. Add links and displayed prices for tiles, showers, toilets, baths, furniture and accessories. The assistant can help identify possible missing parts and questions to check.

Can the AI diagnose everything from a photograph?

No. A photo can help identify visible fittings, general access and layout, but it cannot prove hidden pipework, drainage, structure, waterproofing or exact dimensions.

Is the generated image the design I will receive?

It is an illustrative concept based on the entered brief. It is useful for style and direction, but final layout and product fit are confirmed after survey.

What happens after the planner?

Send the plan or arrange a home survey. We check the room, discuss the specification and prepare the written scope and quotation.
