Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as a new bathroom?+

No. A refresh retains much of the existing room and targets selected visible or functional items. It is only appropriate where the underlying bathroom is in suitable condition.

Can every bathroom be refreshed?+

No. Active leaks, failed substrates, extensive mould, poor drainage or unsafe electrics may mean a fuller renovation is the sensible option.
