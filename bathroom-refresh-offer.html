Before booking a survey

Does the £3,000 bathroom refresh include a new bath, basin or WC?

No. The package is designed for bathrooms where the existing bath, basin and WC remain in place. It focuses on refreshing the visible areas listed in the offer.

Is floor tiling included?

No. Floor tiling or new flooring is not included in the £3,000 package. It can be discussed separately if the room needs it.

Does every bathroom qualify?

No. The free survey checks the existing bathroom condition, access, tiles, walls and fittings. If extra preparation or repair work is needed, it will be explained and priced separately before anything is booked.
