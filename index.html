Have a spare room?

Could a private en-suite help it earn more?

For many London renters, a private shower room changes how a bedroom is compared. In one genuine London example experienced by our founder, an en-suite helped the room achieve around £200 more per month than comparable shared-bathroom accommodation.

That is one individual experience, not a rent guarantee. The result depends on the postcode, room size, remaining bedroom space, finish and current local demand.

Installation-led design

Many companies start with products. We start with the room.

We help shape the layout around your ideas, but the hidden work must be checked first.

01

Measure the real space

Room size, access, doors, windows, walls and floor levels affect what can actually fit.

02

Check water and drainage

Soil pipe positions, waste falls, hot water supply, pressure and flow rate influence the design.

03

Plan the complete build

Preparation, waterproofing, ventilation, electrics, tiling and finishing are considered together.

Builder-led experience

The finished room matters, but so does everything behind it. We plan the drainage, pipe routes, ventilation, preparation, waterproofing, floor levels and installation details that make the design work in a real property.
