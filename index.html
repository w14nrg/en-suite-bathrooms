Many companies start with products. We start with the room.

With thousands of tiles, baths, showers, taps, vanities and bathroom suites available online and in showrooms, it can be difficult to know what will actually work in your home.

We help bring the project together properly. Whether you already have products in mind or are starting from scratch, we look at the room, the services, the layout and the practical installation details before finalising the plan.

Measured properly

Room size, layout, access, walls, floors, pipe routes and existing services all affect the final design.

Plumbing checked

Drainage, waste positions, water pressure and hot water supply need to be understood before products are chosen.

Practical details

Ventilation, electrics, boxing, shower tray options and floor levels all shape what can be built.

Bathroom design that understands the build

Before we design, we check

Bathrooms are rarely simple. Awkward layouts, old pipework, tight spaces, uneven floors, shared drainage, small windows and limited access all need proper planning before anyone starts ripping tiles off.

We combine design ideas with practical installation knowledge, so the finished bathroom is planned around the real room from the beginning.

Room & layout

Measurements, layout, access, doors, windows, boxing and realistic space planning.

Water & drainage

Soil pipe positions, waste runs, water pressure, hot water supply and pipe routes.

Floors & walls

Floor levels, shower tray options, wall preparation, tile choice and waterproofing needs.

Ventilation & electrics

Extractor options, lighting, electrical zones and practical details that affect the finished room.
