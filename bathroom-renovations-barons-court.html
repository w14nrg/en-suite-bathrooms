BATHROOM RENOVATIONS · BARONS COURT W14

Bathroom Renovations in Barons Court

Barons Court is a few minutes from our Fulham shop. Most of the homes here are flats, so we plan the work around neighbours, communal stairs and the building’s rules.

WhatsApp Nicholas

Homes in Barons Court

Barons Court is dominated by mansion blocks and Victorian houses converted into flats. A bathroom here is rarely just about the room itself: there is a flat below, a shared stairwell and often a managing agent to satisfy.

Barons Court work is usually a complete flat bathroom, a shower room upgrade, or a bath replaced with a walk-in shower to free up space.

Things we check first

  • Waterproofing comes first. A leak into the flat below costs far more than doing the tanking properly.
  • Communal stairs and hallways are protected before anything is carried through, and waste is taken out the same day.
  • Leasehold flats often need the freeholder’s consent (a licence to alter) before a layout change. We provide drawings and details for that.
  • Small bathrooms are common, so wall-hung WCs, concealed cisterns and compact basins help the room feel bigger.

Parking and access

Parking in W14 is controlled by Hammersmith & Fulham Council. We book visitor permits or bay suspensions in advance for the strip-out and delivery days.

We visit to survey, measure and check the services, then give you a written quote. Bring photographs to the shop at 287 Munster Road if you would like to talk it through first.

Questions

Do you deal with the managing agent?

We can supply what they normally request, such as a description of works, insurance details and a programme, so you are not left translating between us.

Will the work disturb neighbours?

The noisiest stage is the strip-out, usually the first day or two. We keep to the building’s permitted hours and protect shared areas.

Kitchens, renovations and building work in Barons Court

In Barons Court mansion flats we often pair a bathroom with a kitchen refresh and making good after older wiring is replaced.

Kitchens

Full kitchen renovations: strip-out, new services, units, worktops (Silestone, quartz and other stone) and the tiling and flooring to finish. In mansion-block flats the kitchen is often narrow, so layout and storage are planned carefully, and we work to the building’s rules on noise and access.

Part and full renovations

From a few rooms done together to a full strip-back, rewire, re-plumb, plaster, floor and decorate. In converted flats we check what is shared with the flats around you before any walls, floors or pipes are opened.
