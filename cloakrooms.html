Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need planning permission for a downstairs toilet?+

Often not, but Building Regulations normally apply to drainage, ventilation and electrical work. Listed buildings, leasehold properties and major external alterations may need extra approvals.

Can a cloakroom fit under stairs?+

Sometimes. Head height, door access, waste route and usable clearances must be checked before committing to the layout.
