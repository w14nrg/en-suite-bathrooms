Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you design and install complete bathrooms?+

Yes. We help shape the layout and specification around the actual room, then coordinate rip-out, preparation, plumbing, electrics, waterproofing, tiling, fitting and finishing.

Can I choose products myself?+

Yes. You can bring products or ideas from any retailer. We check dimensions, compatibility, installation requirements and whether the choices work together.

How much does a London bathroom renovation cost?+

It varies widely with room condition, layout changes, preparation and specification. A site survey and agreed scope are needed for a dependable quotation.

Do you work in managed flats?+

Yes, where the project is suitable. Building rules, working hours, access, parking, protection, water isolation and waste removal should be established before work starts.

Start with the real room

Let us check what can actually be built.

Send photographs and a rough description, visit our Fulham consultation office, or arrange a home survey. We will assess space, drainage, water, ventilation and the practical build route before the design is finalised.
