Before you message us

  • Take a photo of the leak or fitting, and one from further back.
  • Find your stopcock, usually under the kitchen sink or where the water pipe enters the house.
  • If something is leaking, turn the water off at the stopcock or the small valve under the fitting.
  • Tell us your postcode so we can confirm we cover it.
