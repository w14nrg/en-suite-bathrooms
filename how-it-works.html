OUR PROCESS

From your ideas to a room that can actually be built.

A clear survey-led process for bathrooms, en-suites and cloakrooms, with practical decisions made before the installation starts.

From first contact to handover

The design is developed around what can actually be built, then the work is sequenced and managed properly.

01

Initial conversation

Tell us the type of room, location, rough budget, timing and ideas you have already seen.

02

Free home survey

We measure and inspect the room, access, water, drainage, ventilation and practical build options.

03

Buildable proposal

The layout, products and finish are brought together around your property, not a showroom plan.

04

Clear quotation

The written quote sets out the agreed scope, allowances and payment stages.

05

Managed installation

Preparation, plumbing, electrics, waterproofing, tiling, fitting and finishing are coordinated.

06

Final checks

The room is tested, finishing details are checked and the project is handed over.

07

Aftercare

Our installation workmanship is guaranteed for 12 months from completion. Product and manufacturer warranties remain separate.
